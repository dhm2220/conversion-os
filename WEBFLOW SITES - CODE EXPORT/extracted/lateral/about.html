Frequently Asked Questions

What is Section 321 Fulfillment and why does it matter?

Section 321 allows U.S. businesses to import goods under $800 duty-free, cutting logistics costs dramatically while optimizing shipping from Mexico to the U.S. Visit the resources page in order to learn everything you need to know about Section 321 fulfillment.

Is shipping slower or less reliable from Mexico?

Delivery times are similar to U.S. fulfillment centers, often 1 to 3 days, with reliable operations ensuring efficiency and security near the U.S. border. Our facilities have 24/7 monitoring, advanced security systems, and strict operational standards to ensure your products are safe.

How long does it take to get up and running with you?

The implementation time for each new partner varies depending on complexity but typically ranges from 3 to 12 weeks. To get the most accurate timeline, schedule a free consultation.

Can I use my own carrier agreements?

Yes. If you would like to share more information, request a free quote or schedule a free consultation.

Do you manage returns?

Yes. Any returns are sent back to the nearest US Cross-Dock location and either recycled or brought back to the delivery center for refurbishment and re-sale.

What value added services do you offer?

Lateral is the world’s only fulfillment partner with in-house customs brokerage and section 321 freight fulfillment capabilities allowing for fast, affordable and flexible fulfillment from manufacturers anywhere in the world. In addition to this, we bring 100+ years of front line industry experience that helps us both advise our partners on best practices and implement end to end technology for even the most complex partner cases.
